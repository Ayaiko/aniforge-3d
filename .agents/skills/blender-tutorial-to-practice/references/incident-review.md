# Incident: premature tutorial transfer

This is a project-specific example, not a rule that every Blender task requires tutorial review. Read the [full Thai retrospective](/Users/graind/Documents/ChatGPT/vTuber/artifacts/anime-face-full-review/REFLECTION.md) for raw evidence and scope.

## Causal findings

- The initial pass used 33 sampled stills and selected captions before coding three skills and modifying a checkpoint copy. It omitted adequate chronological review. Correction: distinguish download status from inspected coverage; disclose the visual method. The later review inspected 1,577 chronological frames in 53 sheets and complete available captions for the requested first three videos. It was not continuous playback.
- A cheek relaxation helper and whole-eyeball translation were authored adaptations. Neither reproduced the artist's sculpt strokes or the eye tutorial's pupil recession. Correction: label technique provenance and what a test actually exercised.
- An absolute RGB classifier missed the green-coded eye after an AgX display transform. This was a new measurement defect, not a Blender failure. Correction: per-object positive controls before scoring and separate measurement passes from artistic display. Historical single-eye results are superseded, not erased.
- Two overconnected edges existed in the original head. Inspection identified opposed duplicate triangle flaps at the neck cap. Removing both faces of each flap eliminated the two defects without moving retained vertices. Intentional eye/mouth boundaries remained unchanged. This is structural repair, not proof of likeness.
- A later chin trial matched the side landmark better and kept the front silhouette, but introduced an abrupt chin mound in front/oblique shading. It was rejected. Local metrics and topology validity could not approve that surface.
